# Assumptions

1. `Martyrs_Foundation_Master_Enterprise_Blueprint_v2_Final.md` was located and reviewed in full. It is the controlling scope reference; ERP/HR screens are tagged to sections 18 and 19.
2. Arabic names, identifiers, rules, legal-reference IDs, organizations, dates, service counts, fees, and percentages are fictional and synthetic.
3. `LR-EDU-*`, `EDU-ELIG-*`, and related records are explainability placeholders, not Iraqi legislation.
4. The educational grant carries no fee in the primary story. The certificate fee is isolated and explicitly labelled as invalid for real collection.
5. Enrollment evidence is considered verified immediately after the citizen selects a local file so the end-to-end story remains demoable without an external document service.
6. Three present, non-conflicted members constitute demo quorum. This is a configuration example, not a legal rule.
7. Two approval votes plus quorum are sufficient to activate the simulated signature in the seeded meeting. This is not committee law or policy.
8. Application state and role are intentionally stored on one device to preserve the live presentation across persona switches.
9. Governorate results use a ranked accessible table instead of an inaccurate map.
10. Public verification checks the locally published synthetic decision only; it does not establish authenticity outside this POC.
11. The operational role selector demonstrates RBAC and segregation of duties but is not production authentication or authorization. Production requires IAM, server-side policy enforcement and individual employee identities.
12. ERP/HR records are synthetic and stored as a typed POC state document in D1. They do not implement statutory accounting, payroll, taxation, period closing, concurrency, bank settlement or a production immutable ledger.
13. The configurable 70/20/10 distribution is a demo rule inherited from the prior POC story and is not asserted as an approved legal allocation.
