# POC Scope

## Included vertical slice

- Arabic-first public service discovery and detail
- Persistent one-device demo persona switching
- Education grant wizard with verified prefill, conditional field, autosave, validation, document simulation, acknowledgement, and submission
- Explicit application state machine and audit events
- Staff inbox and unified case workspace
- Beneficiary 360, Family 360, document review, deterministic eligibility, legal references, communications, tasks, and audit
- Grounded deterministic AI classification, missing-evidence check, summary, explanation, next action, legal references, and duplicate alert
- Citizen completion loop
- Committee attendance, conflict declaration, quorum, votes, reasoned decision, simulated signature, and publication
- Citizen decision timeline and public document verification
- Manager and executive dashboards with synthetic drill-downs
- Interactive form ordering, workflow-node inspection, readable rules, bilingual templates, integration health, and version lifecycle
- Payment sandbox with fee breakdown, fictional token, webhook outcomes, idempotency, receipt, distribution preview, and reconciliation
- ERP role workspaces and task inboxes for nine internal roles
- Transactional screen contract across 17 financial and 18 employee-administration modules: list, search/filter/sort, detail, validated create form, role actions, status and history
- Funds A–D, budget availability, balanced demo journals, enterprise idempotency and segregation of duties
- Connected grant → beneficiary payment → GL → audit and leave → manager approval → balance → payroll → GL/AP stories

## Represented extension domains

Health, housing and land, employment, legal assistance, benefits, certificates, complaints/appeals, appointments/queues, revenue distribution, data quality, and wider governorate operations are represented through catalog entries, navigation, dashboards, seeded records, or preview screens.

## Explicitly excluded production capabilities

- Real identity, authentication, MFA, roles, or authorization enforcement
- Real beneficiary, family, martyr, injured-person, or national registry data
- Legally approved eligibility, entitlement, fee, revenue, or SLA rules
- Real payments, card/bank capture, accounting posting, reconciliation, refunds, payroll, or Martyrs Fund transactions; the ERP/HR operations are synthetic POC state transitions only
- Government, SMS, email, education, banking, or signature connections
- Legally valid committee minutes, decision, signature, certificate, or QR registry
- Multi-user concurrency, production workflow runtime, case assignment, records management, and retention
- Production encryption, immutable ledger, SIEM, secrets management, disaster recovery, observability, and security accreditation
- Open-ended AI, model hosting, evaluation, or personal-data processing
